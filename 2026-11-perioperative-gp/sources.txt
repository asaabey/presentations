SOURCE REGISTER
Checked 17 November 2026. Reference numbers match the lesson explanations.
Primary sources are used; Australian guidance is prioritised where available.

1. ANZCA: GLP-1 receptor agonists – clinical practice recommendations and FAQs.
https://www.anzca.edu.au/safety-and-advocacy/standards-of-practice/clinical-practice-recommendations-regarding-patients-taking-glp-1
The current page includes a fasting clarification updated April 2026 and shows
a last-modified date of 22 October 2026. Used for the Australian GLP-1 case;
do not substitute the older blanket weekly-drug cessation approach.
Companion multisociety recommendations (2025):
https://doi.org/10.1177/0310057X251355288

2. Tran HA et al. 2025 Guidelines for direct oral anticoagulants: a practical
guidance on the prescription, laboratory testing, peri-operative and bleeding
management. Internal Medicine Journal 2025;55:1174–1183.
https://doi.org/10.1111/imj.70103
Locate: Practical summary for perioperative interruption; perioperative
bridging; neuraxial anaesthesia; resumption after surgery.
Used for the apixaban calendar and postoperative distinction between
therapeutic anticoagulation and thromboprophylaxis.

3. Buchbinder R et al. Australian recommendations on perioperative use of
disease-modifying anti-rheumatic drugs in people with inflammatory arthritis
undergoing elective surgery. Internal Medicine Journal 2023;53:1248–1255.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10946831/
https://doi.org/10.1111/imj.16073
Locate: Recommendations and practical considerations. Used to explain why
the biologic case starts with a shared decision rather than a universal hold.

4. Goodman SM et al. 2022 ACR/AAHKS guideline for perioperative management of
antirheumatic medication in patients undergoing elective total hip or knee
arthroplasty. Arthritis & Rheumatology 2022;74:1464–1473.
https://doi.org/10.1002/art.42140
Locate: Tables 2–3. Used for the joint-replacement example, adalimumab interval,
methotrexate continuation and wound-based restart. Its scope is hip/knee
arthroplasty; the recommendations are conditional.

5. Australian Diabetes Society and ANZCA. Perioperative Diabetes and
Hyperglycaemia Guidelines (Adults), November 2022.
https://www.diabetessociety.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/ADS-ANZCA-Perioperative-Diabetes-and-Hyperglycaemia-Guidelines-Adults-November-2022-v2-Final.pdf
Locate: Section 6.1.2; Appendix L, ADS–ANZCA–ADEA–NZSSD SGLT2 alert.
Used for the Australian inpatient-surgery example and metabolic safety advice.
The case uses a calendar, since 'three days' can otherwise be misunderstood.

6. AHA/ACC and partner societies. 2026 Guideline for Perioperative
Cardiovascular Management for Noncardiac Surgery, reaffirmed September 2026.
https://www.jacc.org/doi/full/10.1016/j.jacc.2026.06.017
Locate: Section 7.2, Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System Inhibitors.
Used for the indication-dependent ACE inhibitor/ARB discussion.
Official reaffirmation notice:
https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/2026-guideline-for-perioperative-cardiovascular-management-for-noncardiac-surgery
The notice states that the 2026 reaffirmation retains the 2024 recommendations.
